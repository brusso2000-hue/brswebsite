Commercial Moving: Things to Look For

February 19, 2021 · Business Relocation Services

Hiring moving companies is never really a straightforward ordeal. There’s so much that could go wrong. It’s extremely important that you find a moving company that is reliable and will treat your inventory responsibly. If you’re on this page, it means that you’re looking for a commercial moving company to help you out. Here are a few features that your moving company should always have.

Accommodation for Your Inventory

It is possible that you might require some specialized services for the products that you’re going to be moving. You can’t exactly move a car in a truck that is meant to move furniture, now can you? Talk to the commercial moving company before hiring them to make sure that they have the transporting facilities required to accommodate your belongings.

Ask For Commercial Moving Reviews

Before you start working with a moving company, it is important that you do your research on them. If you have friends and family who have worked with the company in the past, then you’re good to go. Check on the internet for reviews on the company. If they have negative reviews then it might be a good idea to go for some other business instead. Positive reviews are necessary to ensure that they are a credible business and are reliable enough to be your designated commercial moving company.

Commercial Moving Insurance

You should never use a moving company that isn’t insured. This is a difference between a decent moving company and a person in a truck that just wants a few extra dollars. Companies that are licensed are insured and have the legal responsibility to take good care of your property. Moreover, this also gives you the peace of mind that if the company does damage to your inventory even by accident, they’ll be the ones responsible for it.

Beware of Illegitimate Movers

While there’s a 90% chance that you’re going to find a legit moving company, there’s also a chance that you don’t. A very small portion of companies around the world are fraudulent businesses that are functioning without a license or any government approval. To make sure a company is credible, do some digging to see if they have an active license. If they are registered with a government organization, you’re good to go. If they aren’t, check to see if they have a reputation online and if they have a valid physical office. Moreover, if the commercial moving company asks for a deposit of more than 20%, that is a red flag. Any legit business isn’t going to ask you to pay more than a 20% safety deposit.
