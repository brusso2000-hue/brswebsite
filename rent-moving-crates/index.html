Rent reusable plastic moving crates for your office move

Commercial-grade plastic crates, delivered clean and collected when your move is done. No cardboard to buy, assemble, tape or dispose of.

Request a Free QuoteCall 1-718-399-8000

Business Relocation Services (BRS) rents reusable plastic moving crates to offices, schools, healthcare organizations and other businesses. Crates arrive clean and ready to use, and we collect them once your move is complete.

Plastic crates are sturdy, stackable and weather resistant. They protect documents, electronics and equipment, and they remove the work of buying, assembling, taping and disposing of cardboard boxes.

Rentals are planned around your timeline, from a few days to several weeks. We help you work out how many crates you need and when.

Why choose BRS

  • Faster, more organized moves

    Labeled, stackable crates speed up packing and unpacking and make it easier to sort departments, files and equipment. Teams settle into the new space sooner.

  • Less waste

    Reusable crates reduce waste compared with disposable cardboard. Each crate can be reused many times.

  • Flexible rental terms

    Short-term and long-term rentals, with quantities based on the size of your move.

What is included

  • Heavy-duty, commercial-grade plastic crates
  • Secure, interlocking lids
  • Uniform sizing for easy stacking
  • Smooth interiors and moisture and weather resistance
  • Cleaned and sanitized crates before delivery
  • Delivery and pickup scheduling
  • Custom quantities based on move size
  • Help estimating how many crates you need

How it works

  1. Estimate quantities

    Tell us about your offices, staff or rooms. We help estimate how many crates you need and when.

  2. Schedule delivery

    We deliver clean, ready-to-use crates to your location on the agreed date.

  3. Pack and label

    Your team packs and labels crates by department, file or equipment type.

  4. Collect after the move

    Once the move is complete, we pick the crates up. Nothing to assemble or dispose of.

Frequently asked questions

How many moving crates do I need?

It depends on the size of your move. We can help estimate the quantity based on the number of offices, employees or rooms involved.

How long can I rent crates for?

Rental periods are flexible, from a few days to several weeks, with short-term and long-term options.

Are the crates cleaned before delivery?

Yes. All crates are cleaned and sanitized before delivery.

Do you deliver in New York City?

Yes. We deliver and collect crates throughout New York City and surrounding areas. We also have partner warehouses in 19 locations nationally. For pricing, email rent_crates@brsrelocations.com.
