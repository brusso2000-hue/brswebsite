Frequently Asked Questions – Rent Moving Crates

Here are some frequently asked questions (FAQs) about Eco-Friendly Moving Crate Rentals in New York.

What are moving crates and how do they work?

Moving crates are reusable plastic containers designed for safe and efficient packing. When you rent moving crates from Business Relocation Services, we deliver clean, ready-to-use crates to your location and pick them up once your move is complete.

Why should I rent moving crates instead of cardboard boxes?

Renting moving crates saves time and money. Plastic crates are sturdier, stackable, weather-resistant, and reusable. Unlike cardboard boxes, they don’t require assembly or disposal after your move.

How many moving crates do I need?

The number of crates depends on the size of your move. Our team can help estimate the right quantity based on the number of offices, employees, or rooms involved in your relocation.

Are your moving crates suitable for office relocations?

Yes. Our crates are ideal for office and business moves, providing protection for documents, electronics, and equipment while keeping everything organized and labeled.

How long can I rent moving crates for?

We offer flexible rental periods, including short-term and long-term options. Whether you need crates for a few days or several weeks, we tailor the rental to your schedule.

Are the moving crates cleaned before delivery?

Absolutely. All crates are professionally cleaned and sanitized before delivery to ensure they are safe, hygienic, and ready to use.

Do you deliver moving crates in New York City?

Yes. Business Relocation Services provides moving crate delivery and pickup throughout New York City and surrounding areas. We also have partner warehouses in 19 locations nationally.

Are moving crates environmentally friendly?

Yes. Renting reusable plastic crates significantly reduces waste and supports eco-friendly moving practices.
