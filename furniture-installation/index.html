Office furniture installation and assembly

BRS plans, delivers, assembles and cleans up after your office furniture project, so your team can get back to their workstations quickly.

Request a Free QuoteCall 1-718-399-8000

Installation is one of the most important parts of an office furniture project, and it should be planned together with furniture selection. BRS helps you plan and organize new installations or reconfigure your current space.

Our project managers and installers handle delivery, assembly and clean-up. The goal is minimal interference with your office's productivity.

Why choose BRS

  • Planned with your selection

    Installation is scheduled alongside your furniture choices, not left until the last minute.

  • Less lost productivity

    Employees are often displaced during a project. We work to get them back to their workstations as soon as possible.

  • One team on site

    BRS can bring IT personnel and electricians in to get the space up and running.

What is included

  • Planning and organizing new furniture installations
  • Reconfiguration of your current office space
  • Delivery of new furniture
  • Assembly by experienced installers
  • IT personnel to connect equipment
  • Electricians where the project needs them
  • Clean-up after installation

How it works

  1. Plan the install

    We plan the installation with your furniture selection and the layout of your space.

  2. Deliver and assemble

    Our installers deliver and assemble the furniture in a coordinated sequence.

  3. Connect and clean up

    IT and electrical staff get the workstations running, and we clear the space so staff can return.
