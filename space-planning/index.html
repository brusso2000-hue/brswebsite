Office space planning for relocations and reconfigurations

BRS organizes space, furniture and functions to work together, so your office makes efficient use of the room you have.

Request a Free QuoteCall 1-718-399-8000

Space planning organizes the space, furniture and functions of an office so they work together and meet the needs of your employees and clients.

Whether you are relocating to a new space, expanding or consolidating a facility, or making cosmetic changes, BRS provides practical solutions for efficient use of space.

Why choose BRS

  • Efficient use of space

    Layouts are built around how your groups actually work.

  • Fewer surprises later

    Planning accounts for building codes, lighting, storage and communication needs up front.

  • Coordinated execution

    BRS also handles moves, furniture and IT coordination, so the plan carries through.

What is included

  • Planning around workgroup function and teaming requirements
  • Review of building codes and regulations
  • Lighting and storage considerations
  • Inter-communication needs between groups
  • Planning for relocation and design of a new space
  • Planning for expansion or consolidation
  • Reconfiguration of existing space

How it works

  1. Understand the needs

    We review workgroups, workflow and the constraints of the space.

  2. Plan the layout

    We organize furniture and functions to fit the space, within code and building requirements.

  3. Coordinate the change

    Reconfigurations disrupt staff, so we coordinate with IT and facilities to carry out the changes.

Frequently asked questions

What does space planning take into account?

Workgroup function, building codes and regulations, lighting, teaming requirements, inter-communication and storage.

Why do reconfigurations need coordination?

They can be complex and disruptive to staff, and involve a chain of events that includes IT and facilities.

What types of projects do you plan for?

Relocation and design of a new space, expansion or consolidation of an existing facility, and cosmetic enhancements.
