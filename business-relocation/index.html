One accountable team for the entire move

Business relocation is more than trucks and boxes. It requires facilities planning, building coordination, IT sequencing, furniture installation, inventory control and a team that can adapt on moving day. BRS brings those disciplines together under one project manager, from initial scope through final closeout.

BRS was established in 1987 and is well known in both corporate and government circles. Our clients include corporations, small to large businesses, city agencies and non-profits.

How BRS manages a relocation

  1. Assess and scope

    Walk the space, document inventory, confirm building requirements and identify risks.

  2. Plan and schedule

    Build the move plan, phasing, staffing, equipment needs and communication timeline.

  3. Coordinate every party

    Align facilities, building management, IT, furniture vendors and internal teams.

  4. Move, install and close out

    Execute the move, complete installation, resolve the punch list and confirm completion.
