Secure business storage with full item tracking

BRS stores your boxes, files and equipment in a managed facility and tracks the movement and access history of every item. Storage that fits around your move.

Request a Free QuoteCall 1-718-399-8000

Business Relocation Services (BRS) offers secure storage for companies that need somewhere safe to keep property during a move, a renovation or a longer transition. Our facility lets us track, manage and secure your most important items.

Tracking software records the movement and access history of every box, file or item you place in our care. You always know what is stored, where it is and who has accessed it.

Storage works alongside our moving services, so items can go from your office to the facility and back again under one plan.

Why choose BRS

  • Security you can verify

    Security is the most important factor in a storage facility. Our focus is on protecting what you entrust to us and giving you confidence in how it is handled.

  • Every item tracked

    Our software records the movement and access history of each box, file or item. Nothing is stored without a record.

  • Flexible storage options

    We offer flexible moving and storage options, so you can fit storage to your project instead of the other way round.

What is included

  • Secure storage in a large, managed facility
  • Software tracking of each box, file or item
  • Movement and access history for everything stored
  • Storage for boxes, files and other business property
  • Flexible moving and storage options
  • Staff attention around the clock
  • Coordination with your move, so items travel and return under one plan

How it works

  1. Plan the storage

    We review what needs to be stored, for how long and when it will be needed again.

  2. Move and log items

    Items are moved to the facility and entered in our tracking software.

  3. Store and monitor

    We keep your property secure and record any movement or access.

  4. Return on schedule

    When you are ready, we bring items back to your new space as part of the move.
