Frequently asked questions

Why is moving servers considered risky?

If something goes wrong, data can be lost, and there are security risks as well. That is why careful planning matters.

Why create an inventory list?

A data center has hundreds of components, and it is easy to overlook one. Auditing the list two to three times and checking items off on move day helps avoid that.

Why does labeling matter for a server move?

Cables and equipment need to be reinstalled correctly. Labeling makes unpacking and reinstalling easier.
