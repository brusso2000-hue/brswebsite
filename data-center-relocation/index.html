Frequently asked questions

Why does documentation matter in a data center move?

IT infrastructure is complex, and few people can rebuild it from memory. Written records show where cables and equipment belong.

Who should lead a data center relocation?

An experienced moving company is the best option. If your IT team does it, assign a project manager experienced in taking apart and rebuilding a data center.

What does the rest of the organization need to do?

Departments should give the relocation team the tools and communication it needs so obstacles are cleared quickly.
