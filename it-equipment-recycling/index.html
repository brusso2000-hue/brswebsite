Benefits of IT Equipment Recycling

Every few years, an organization has to completely upgrade its entire IT infrastructure simply because the old system is now obsolete. In order to maintain a productive work environment, a company has to upgrade its systems after 3-5 years. That’s a perfectly logical thing to do. However, the problem is that you’re throwing away the old systems.

Just tossing everything in the garbage seems like a very wasteful use of all that hardware. So, what should you do with it? The answer is to recycle it all. If you’re not familiar with IT equipment recycling, here’s why IT equipment recycling is a good idea.

Reduce Waste in Landfill

By recycling equipment, the very first benefit you’re doing to the environment is reducing the waste in landfills. Global landfills are increasing at a rapid rate, and chucking old but reusable equipment in these landfills is a terrible waste of resources. You’re going to be keeping technology out of that landfill and in the hands of people that have use for it.

Toxin Pollution

A computer sitting on your bed is absolutely harmless. However, when a ton of computers are placed in a landfill, there’s a huge risk of toxin pollution. Computer chips contain chemicals like mercury, beryllium, and cadmium in small quantities.

One computer doesn’t have enough of them to pose a threat. However, when you start piling up endless tech in your landfills, you’re releasing toxins in water and the air.

Electronics Donation and Recycling

The current hardware in your office might be outdated for your use. However, there are places out there that could use your devices. A good example of that would be schools in Africa. They don’t have the luxury of computers, let alone outdated ones.

What’s old, discarded tech for you is a blessing for them. So, when you contact some IT recycling company, you can ask them to get your computer in the hands of deserving people – People who could really use your devices.

Secure Data Disposal

Even if you completely delete everything off a hard drive, that doesn’t mean that everything on the hard drive is gone. Hard drives still retain old data, which is possible to retrieve with a data recovery tool. That’s where an IT recycling company comes in.

Before they do anything with your hardware, they go through your data storage devices to carefully wipe off anything personal you might have on them.
