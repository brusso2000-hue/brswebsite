Frequently asked questions

Is deleting files enough before recycling a hard drive?

No. Deleted data can still be retrieved with recovery tools, so storage devices need to be wiped or destroyed.

Why not throw old IT equipment in the trash?

It adds to landfill waste, and electronics contain chemicals such as mercury, beryllium and cadmium that can reach water and air.

Can working equipment be donated?

It can be. Working equipment may go to people who can use it, instead of being discarded.
